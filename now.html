Now | August 2026

What has my
attention.

Deep August on the coast. The heat is at its peak, the kind that makes everything feel slightly unreal between noon and four. South Texas in summer has a way of slowing down the surface while the undercurrent keeps moving, and that is where I am right now, quiet on the outside, a lot turning over underneath. I have been thinking about what I am actually building, not just operating, and the answer is taking shape in a way that feels worth following.

In progress.

The KDP book series. Book 1 is at the finish line, which means the real work is just beginning. Uploading, reviewing, pricing, positioning. I am thinking about how to introduce this to the right audience without overexplaining it, which is harder than writing the book itself. The answer is probably just to ship it and see what happens.

Also working through what the next six months look like for the client side of the business. Not adding headcount, not chasing volume. I want better problems. The infrastructure decisions I am most proud of came from clients who trusted me to think, not just execute. Finding more of those is the project right now.

Paying attention to.

Water rights in South Texas. It keeps coming up in ways that feel underreported. Agriculture, population growth, the petrochemical corridor, all of it pulling from the same shrinking supply. I am not an expert, but I live here and I pay attention, and what I am seeing does not match the confidence in the official projections.

Where AI actually lands in small business operations over the next two years. Not the hype, not the fear, the actual friction points where it either solves something real or gets in the way. I am close enough to small and medium businesses to watch this play out in real time, which makes it worth following carefully.

August heat. Even still water has somewhere to go.
